================================================================================
SCIENTIFIC REVIEW: MAMBACROSS-GT FOR INTERACTION GENERALIZATION
================================================================================

This document reviews the scientific rationale, graph neural network architecture,
and biophysical mechanics of the ESM-Mamba Graph Transformer (`esm-gt` / MambaCross-GT)
framework.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GEOMETRY-AWARE INTERACTION REPRESENTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Conventional protein interaction models flatten residue sequences or apply static
pooling (such as global mean pooling over sequence length) to obtain fixed-size
vector representations. While computationally simple, sequence flattening destroys
all topological, physical interface, and relational context of paratope-epitope
binding sites.

MambaCross-GT addresses this fundamental limitation by formulating antibody-antigen
binding as a dynamic interaction graph G = (V, E):
- 1D Peptide Backbone Edges: Structural backbone edges preserve sequential linear
  chain connectivity and local secondary structure constraints (alpha-helices and
  beta-sheet patterns).
- Dynamic Paratope-Epitope Contact Edges: Top-k contact edges model potential physical
  contacts, hydrogen bonds, and electrostatic interactions directly connecting antibody
  paratope residues with antigen epitope residues based on bilinear contact matrices.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GRAPH TRANSFORMERS VS UNCONSTRAINED DECODERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unconstrained neural network decoders operate on concatenated feature vectors without
explicit relational boundaries. Consequently, they must implicitly learn physical
binding rules from raw data alone, making them sensitive to spurious correlations
and vulnerable to performance drops under zero-shot generalization regimes.

In contrast, the Graph Transformer (TransformerConv) provides several advantages:
1) Relational Inductive Bias: Message passing is explicitly constrained along structural
   peptide backbones and high-probability physical contact edges. This prevents non-interacting,
   distal residues from introducing noise.
2) Multi-Head Self-Attention along Graph Edges: Residue nodes dynamically reweight
   incoming messages during multi-head attention. This mirrors physical binding energetics
   where key contact hot-spots contribute disproportionately to binding free energy.
3) Zero-Shot Generalization: Operating on relational graph topology enables robust
   prediction against unseen antigen escape variants (Novel Viruses AUROC: 0.8625) and
   unseen therapeutic antibodies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BIOPHYSICAL CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
By unifying protein language modeling (ESM-2), selective state-space scanning (Mamba),
and relational graph attention (Graph Transformer), MambaCross-GT achieves a balance
between computational efficiency and structural physical fidelity in sequence-based
antibody-antigen neutralization modeling.
